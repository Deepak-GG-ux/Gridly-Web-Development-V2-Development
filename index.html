How it works

About this verification service

A short explanation of what a verified result does and does not mean.

What is checked

The ID you enter is compared against the register of internship certificates issued by Gridly. A match returns the full record: name, roll number, role, project, duration, technologies, signatory and issue date.

What it confirms

That Gridly issued a certificate with that ID, and that the details shown are the details on the register. Any discrepancy between a printed certificate and this record means the printed copy should not be relied on.

What it is not

This is not an accreditation body, a university record or a government verification service. It confirms an internship completed on the Gridly website project and nothing beyond that.

Privacy. The lookup runs entirely in your browser against a list held in this page's JavaScript. The ID you type is not transmitted or recorded anywhere.
