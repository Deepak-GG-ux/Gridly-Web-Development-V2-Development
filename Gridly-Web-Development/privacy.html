What this site collects

This website is a static site. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking scripts. Pages are served as files and no visitor profile is built.

The contact form

The contact form is not connected to a mail service in this deployment. When you submit it, the details you typed stay in your browser and are shown back to you so you can copy them or open them in your own email client. Nothing is transmitted to Gridly or to any third party.

If the form is later connected to a form-handling service, the details you submit would be processed by that service. This page would need updating to name the service and describe its retention period.

Information stored in your browser

One preference is stored locally on your device: your chosen colour theme, saved under the key gridly:theme. It never leaves your browser and is not used to identify you. Clearing your site data removes it.

Third-party requests

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which means your browser makes a request to Google's servers when a page loads. No other external service is contacted. To remove this, the font files can be self-hosted from the assets/ folder.

Certificate verification

The verification page checks an ID against a list held in the site's own JavaScript. No lookup is sent anywhere, and the ID you type is not recorded.

Contact about privacy

Questions about this page can be sent through the contact page. Contact details on this site are demonstration values for a portfolio project.
