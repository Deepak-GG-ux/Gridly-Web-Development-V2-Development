Principles

What guides each stage

The process is a sequence of steps. These are the principles that decide how each step is carried out.

    Working together

    What we need from you

    Projects run late for predictable reasons. Three of them are within your control.

    Content, early

    Text and images are the most common cause of delay. Development can proceed against a structure, but nothing launches without the words.

    One decision maker

    Feedback gathered from several people and passed on unfiltered tends to contradict itself. One person consolidating it keeps the project moving.

    Account access

    Domain registrar, DNS and any existing hosting access should be located before launch week rather than during it.
